# Requirements

## Objective

Determine whether the remaining normal-authority gaps are caused by feeding a
discontinuous retained suffix to one SQP linearization, by comparing the
existing time-aligned suffix (B) with a dynamically reachable candidate (C).

## Frozen evidence

- baseline: `7eecb011`;
- rejected production experiment: `output/20260829-185730`;
- both raw and direct time-aligned suffix QPs reached OSQP maximum iterations
  at `steering-rate-prefix` rows;
- the existing nonlinear latest-state projection found a physically connected
  continuation at many of the same observations.

## C-arm contract

- x0 is the exact latest seven-state feedback state;
- the surviving B-arm suffix supplies homotopy, references, hard bounds and
  the prepared input sequence;
- every candidate future state is rebuilt by the canonical nonlinear
  seven-state transition from x0;
- candidate inputs are projected only into existing certified input and
  cumulative steering-prefix bounds;
- the unchanged full problem is relinearized around that reachable rollout;
- all original wall, dynamic-obstacle, terminal and physical proof rows remain;
- this Slice is observation/offline only and cannot reach Store or publisher.

## Prohibited changes

- no production authority change;
- no retry, lease, grace period, timeout or fallback;
- no solver tolerance or iteration change;
- no steering, wall, clearance, speed or behavior tuning;
- no clamping of a final solver result;
- no claim that candidate reachability proves physical feasibility.

## Acceptance

- deterministic tests prove that every C-arm candidate state is generated by
  the canonical nonlinear transition under bounded inputs;
- the exact B problem and its hard rows are preserved except for dynamics
  tangents;
- a frozen counterexample where B rejects and C solves is reproduced, or the
  evidence explicitly refutes candidate generation as the root cause;
- a bounded observation run compares B/C without changing commands.

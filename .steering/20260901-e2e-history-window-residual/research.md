# Research Report

## Summary

- A recent head-to-head F1TENTH imitation-learning result uses a recurrent
  direct policy, not a framewise strided CNN residual.
- Its input retains every LiDAR beam as a spatial-pressure token and adds ego
  speed; a GRU carries temporal state across the whole scenario.
- This matches the local failure evidence more closely than another residual
  loss-weight or fixed-frame sweep.

## Confirmed Facts

- End2Race accepts 360 LiDAR ranges at one-degree resolution and ego speed,
  applies a learnable sigmoid pressure transform, and feeds the result to a
  single-layer GRU.  It outputs speed and steering through an MLP decoder.
- Demonstrations are generated by a lattice planner in 600 eight-second
  overtaking scenarios.  They are downsampled to 10 Hz and each scenario is
  trained as an entire sequence, with loss at every timestep.
- The published evaluation reports 94.2% safety and 59.2% overtaking across
  2,400 scenarios.  The authors' LiDAR-only ablation is less safe than the
  speed-conditioned model.
- The public reference implementation preserves the LiDAR feature dimension in
  the GRU input rather than first reducing angular resolution through the
  TinyLidarNet Conv/stride stack.

Sources:

- [End2Race paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.16894)
- [End2Race implementation](https://github.com/michigan-traffic-lab/End2Race)
- [TinyLidarNet reference implementation](https://github.com/CSL-KU/TinyLidarNet)

## Unconfirmed / TBD

- End2Race uses F1TENTH Gym, 360-degree LiDAR and a non-reactive opponent.  Its
  reported rates cannot be transferred directly to AWSIM or this 180-degree,
  750-beam observation contract.
- Automotive AI Challenge 2026 submission compute limits and permitted runtime
  dependencies still need to be checked against the final official rules.

## Impact on This Repository

- Keep the current production TinyLidarNet frozen as the accepted baseline.
- Build the next candidate offline as a direct recurrent policy with full
  scenario sequences, per-beam pressure tokens and synchronized ego speed.
- Do not retrofit the GRU into the rejected residual head.  Compare it as an
  alternative policy, then require offline, single-car and peer gates before
  authority promotion.
- Add speed provenance and synchronization to a new dataset schema rather than
  mutating existing generated datasets.

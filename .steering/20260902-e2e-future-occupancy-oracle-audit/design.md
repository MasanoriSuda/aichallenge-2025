# Design

For a candidate starting at scan time `t0`, each rollout state at `t0 + dt*k`
uses the nearest recorded future scan and the latest preceding odometry pose.
The scan points are transformed from that future LiDAR frame through world
coordinates and back into the `t0` base-link frame.  The candidate's oriented
rectangular footprint is checked against the time-matched point set.

This is a hindsight oracle, not an online predictor.  It answers whether
time-indexed occupancy contains discriminating supervision before investing in
a recurrent model or learned predictor.  It still cannot certify occluded
geometry and it observes the future generated by the recorded ego trajectory,
not the counterfactual candidate trajectory.

Interpretation:

- failure-only selected-side rejection with opposite viability supports a
  temporal homotopy teacher;
- similar rates in successful runs reject this candidate representation;
- widespread no-candidate frames indicate missing geometry/occlusion rather
  than a reason to relax clearance.

An observed failure-only rate is accepted only when the same frozen candidate
bank certifies at least half of every successful reference run.  This is a
representation-validity gate, not a tunable production threshold: an oracle
that rejects most known-successful behavior cannot supervise a policy.

Production remains untouched.

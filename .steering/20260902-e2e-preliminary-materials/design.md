# Design

The deck separates three evidence classes:

1. qualified production: packaged raw + spatial ML, single/NPC closed loop;
2. diagnostic experiments: useful lessons but not production authority;
3. known limitation: symmetric controllable-peer interaction.

The primary story is not that every interaction is solved.  It is that the
team built a reproducible LiDAR-to-steering ML system, improved it with
outcome-gated data, proved NPC behavior over independent seeds, and created
fail-closed evidence contracts that exposed the remaining peer limitation.

The video checklist selects the packaged-default seed-2037 configuration.  A
fresh recording must pass the same analyzer before it may replace the frozen
evidence.  Visual quality cannot override a penalty or stall rejection.

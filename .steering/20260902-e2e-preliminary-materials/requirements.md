# Requirements

## Objective

Prepare an evidence-linked preliminary E2E slide and video checklist from the
frozen production candidate.

## Constraints

- Cover driving data, other-vehicle avoidance/stop, architecture, training and
  evaluation, and originality.
- Use packaged production evidence for the primary video.
- Label rejected recurrent/teacher/peer experiments as rejected or remaining
  work; never present them as production success.
- Keep team name, screenshots and public-video URL as explicit placeholders.
- Do not change runtime code, model artifacts or launch defaults.

## Definition of Done

- A renderable Marp deck exists under `docs/guide/`.
- Every quantitative claim names its immutable local evidence run.
- A checklist gives an exact clean rerun command and recording acceptance.
- Documentation index links both files.
